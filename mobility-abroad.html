First choice – HTWG Konstanz

Country
Germany (Konstanz)
Organisation
HTWG Konstanz, a university of applied sciences; academic exchange
Why it fits
Engineering programmes consistent with my N7 training, and a teaching model that encourages autonomy and self-organisation, with a balanced number of supervised hours. I have already stayed in Germany and appreciate its rigour and everyday organisation. I recently started learning German, so a semester there would let me consolidate it through full immersion. Konstanz, a dynamic border city on the lake, is a good setting for both work and an intercultural experience. Link with Bruno Ramael’s advice: foreign languages are a competitive edge.

Second choice – University of Namur

Country
Belgium (Namur)
Organisation
University of Namur; one-semester academic exchange through the European alliance UNIVERSEH (space focus)
Why it fits
A renowned Computer Science faculty, with an in-depth and rigorous curriculum that matches my N7 training. I can study and communicate daily in French and English, and Namur offers a human-scale environment suited to studying. The UNIVERSEH scholarship is a major financial asset, for a mobility at the heart of the European space ecosystem. Link with Pascal Traverse: the space and aeronautics world of embedded systems.

Third choice – University of Sherbrooke

Country
Canada (Sherbrooke, Québec)
Organisation
University of Sherbrooke, a partner university of N7, known for engineering excellence, notably in computer science and mechanics
Why it fits
Flexible formulas combining academic courses and company internships, which could extend the mobility up to one year. The francophone and bilingual environment makes the procedures easier while consolidating my professional English. I have long been attracted by Canada, and the campus, a lively university town, is ideal for getting involved in associations and activities. Link with Armand Daraei: practical experience in companies matters before specialised roles.
