My studies

I’m in the first year of the engineering degree in computer science and telecommunications at Toulouse INP–ENSEEIHT, a French grande école d’ingénieurs. Before N7, I completed the first two years of a Computer Science Licence at the University of Toulouse (2024–2026, top 15% in L1 and L2), with a solid grounding in algorithmics, low-level programming, object-oriented programming and mathematics. I hold a Baccalauréat Général with highest honours (mention Très Bien, 2024), with Mathematics, Computer Science (NSI) and Engineering Sciences as specialities. At N7, the possible specialisations include networks/telecom, cybersecurity, data science/AI, embedded systems and Cloud/DevOps.
