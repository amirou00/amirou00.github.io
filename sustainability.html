Sustainability & Civic Engagement

01

My perspective

Beyond my studies, I try to be useful to the people around me and to stay informed about environmental issues. I volunteered for my city’s summer event in 2025, I currently volunteer on Explorejob to help people discover the IT field, and in October 2026 I attended two conferences of the Futurs Proches festival, on biodiversity and on water management on campus.

01

Initiatives & engagement

01

Volunteer at the Été Caouec (Blagnac)

Activity
Volunteering during summer 2025 at the Été Caouec, the summer event of Blagnac, organised by the town hall (mairie) of Blagnac.
My contribution
Welcoming visitors (reception), logistics and general support wherever needed: a versatile role.
Reflection
This event was mainly for children, and it made me understand how such simple a event can make others happy bring people together. It also gave me a sens of responsibility, as I was put in charge of different things on multiple occasions.
02

Explorejob volunteer (ongoing)

Activity
Explorejob is an online platform where I present my academic and professional background, and people exploring a career change can contact me.
My contribution
I answer their questions and talk with them about the IT field.
Reflection
It is interesting how common it is for people to change careers, because most people think that if Your choose a certain career then you will be stuck with it for your whole life, but sometimes the only thing that keeps someone from these changes is the lack of information, and the objective of Explorejob is to eliminate this lack of information.
03

Futurs Proches festival (15 October 2026)

Activity
Two conferences at the University of Toulouse, during the festival of campuses in transition. (1) The change in insect populations caused by light pollution. (2) Water resources on the campus: sanitation and reuse, innovative wastewater treatment, bio-inspired filters, and a discussion with the audience, hosted by the Faculty of Science and Engineering.
My contribution
Attendee of both conferences.
Reflection
These two conferences showed me that environmental problems often have simple, concrete causes, like artificial light disturbing insects, and that solutions can be just as concrete, like bio-inspired filters that treat wastewater. As a future engineer, I realised that sustainability is not only a matter of big policies: it also relies on well-designed, local technical solutions, built by teams from different fields. It made me want to ask myself, in my own projects, what their impact is and wether it is good or bad.
