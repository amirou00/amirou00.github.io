Role model 1

Name
Bruno Ramael
Job
Full-stack and AI developer (N7 alumnus)
Short analysis
Interview on 8 October 2026. It clarified the difference between creating AI models (research, mathematics, training algorithms) and AI software development, which is his core job: integrating, industrialising and interfacing models inside complete, scalable and reliable applications. His daily work is highly collaborative: code reviews, agile methods and constant coordination with data engineers, project managers and clients.
Key takeaways
Three pillars beyond technique: soft skills (adaptability, critical thinking, explaining complex ideas simply); languages (fluent English is essential for code, documentation and international projects, and other languages are a competitive edge); and networking (use the AIN7 alumni directory from the first year to find internships and talk with peers). For me: combine AI skills with solid, complete software engineering and an active professional network.

Role model 2

Name
Pascal Traverse
Job
Retired Airbus engineer, expert in embedded systems and autonomy (N7 alumnus)
Company
Airbus (retired)
Short analysis
Interview on 10 October 2026. After a PhD at LAAS-CNRS and a stay at UCLA, he had a major career at Airbus: design of the A320 electrical flight controls, the A380 assembly line, and the innovation strategy on autonomy. Critical embedded systems sit at a crossroads of mechanics, electronics, control engineering and aeronautics, so an engineer must talk with specialists from other fields to ensure safety and certification. His path shows many possible trajectories: deep technical expertise, global architect, team management and large project leadership.
Key takeaways
Soft skills come first: an engineer with excellent relational and communication skills is often far more effective than a highly specialised expert who cannot work in a group. On AI, he is pragmatic: its long-term future is hard to predict, but today it is an assistant and an accelerator for engineers. For me: build an open engineering culture and develop my interpersonal skills to lead complex projects in teams.

Role model 3

Name
Armand Daraei
Job
Head of pentesting
Company
Airbus
Short analysis
One-hour interview on 23 October 2026 about the cybersecurity ecosystem. There is a clear distinction between the Blue Team (defence) and the Red Team (attack), plus other specialities. Penetration testing is very demanding and rarely a first job: time as a SOC analyst is often needed before offensive roles. Profiles range from Bac+2 to Bac+5, but an engineering degree remains a decisive asset for responsibility and career progression.
Key takeaways
AI is transforming the job by automating simple tasks (generated attacks and defence), which weakens junior positions first and demands fast technical added value. Passion matters most: curiosity and a real interest in the field are the best engine for lifelong learning. For me: master the technical fundamentals (systems, networks) at N7 and choose a direction guided by genuine interest.
