03

BIA – Aeronautical Initiation Certificate (Brevet d’Initiation Aéronautique)

Diploma
A French national certificate organised by the Ministry of Education with partners such as the Air Force and the civil aviation authority (DGAC). It certifies a general culture of aeronautics.
What it covers
Five themes: meteorology and aerology; aerodynamics, aerostatics and the principles of flight; aircraft and space vehicles; navigation, regulations and flight safety; history and culture of aeronautics and space. It is assessed by a national written multiple-choice exam.
Why it matters to me
It gave me a first foundation in aeronautics, a key sector in Toulouse, and it connects to my interest in embedded systems, a field I discussed with Pascal Traverse, a retired Airbus engineer.
